## Why TikTok's own captions don't solve this

TikTok does have automatic captions. Creators enable them at upload, viewers toggle them on, and they work reasonably well in a lot of languages.

They also cannot be exported. There is no copy button, no download, no select-all. They are drawn onto the playback surface. If you want the words as text, the caption feature does not help you — which is why transcription tools work from the audio instead.

This has one useful side effect: transcribing from audio works on **every** public video, including all the ones where the creator never turned captions on.

## The alternatives, ranked honestly

**1. Automatic transcription from the link — seconds.** What this guide describes. Fastest by a wide margin, and free for low volumes.

**2. Download the video, then run Whisper locally — 10 to 30 minutes of setup, then minutes per video.** OpenAI's Whisper is genuinely excellent and free if you are comfortable with Python or a command line. It is the right choice if you are transcribing hundreds of videos on your own hardware and do not mind the plumbing. It is overkill for three TikToks.

**3. Paid human transcription — hours to days, per-minute pricing.** Services like Rev produce near-perfect output. Worth it for legal, medical or broadcast use. Wildly disproportionate for a 40-second clip.

**4. Type it out yourself — 5 to 10x the video length.** Free, perfectly accurate if you are careful, and almost never the right trade. The exception: very short clips with terrible audio, where automatic tools struggle and you would be editing heavily anyway.

## Getting the best accuracy out of it

A few things measurably improve results:

**Pick videos where the voice is louder than the music.** This is the single biggest factor. A trending sound mixed high over the speaker will cost you accuracy no tool can recover.

**Expect to fix proper nouns.** Names, brands and niche jargon are the most common miss across every transcription engine. They are also easy to spot and fix on a read-through.

**Watch for code-switching.** Videos that flip between languages mid-sentence are the hardest case. The output is usually still usable, just messier.

**Don't publish a quote you haven't checked.** Read the specific line back against the video. This applies to every automatic transcription tool ever made.
