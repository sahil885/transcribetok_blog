We make TranscribeTok, so treat this with the scepticism a vendor comparison deserves. WayinVideo is the strongest dedicated video-AI product we compete against on TikTok queries, and on most feature rows it beats us outright. This is the comparison we would want to read if we were choosing.

The short version: **WayinVideo is an AI video platform that produces transcripts along the way. TranscribeTok is a transcript tool and nothing else.** If your output is a finished clip, WayinVideo. If your output is text, the extra machinery is a cost, not a benefit.

## What WayinVideo actually is

WayinVideo (wayin.ai) markets itself as an AI video platform, not a transcription service. Its core features are AI clipping that turns long videos into vertical shorts, AI video search that lets you jump to a moment by typing a question, a summariser that returns a paragraph plus a timestamped outline, transcription with punctuation and speaker labels, an animated caption generator, auto-reframing to 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, and an API for triggering all of it programmatically. It also does visual mind maps, chat-with-the-video, virality scoring, scheduled publishing to connected social accounts, and brand kits.

It accepts links from YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch, Dailymotion, Rumble, Kick, Zoom and Google Drive, plus uploads from your device. It advertises 100+ languages and ships a Chrome extension.

TranscribeTok takes one public TikTok link and returns the words that were spoken. No uploads, no other platforms, no batch, no API, no extension, no editing, no translation. Two transcripts a day, free, with no account. That is the entire product.

## Where WayinVideo is clearly better

**It edits video; we do not.** If you need the clip itself — reframed, subtitled, filler removed, scheduled to post — WayinVideo does the whole job and we do none of it. Our SRT export is an input to your editor, not a finished video.

**Speaker labels and in-app search.** WayinVideo separates speakers and lets you search the transcript by keyword and jump to the timestamp. On a multi-speaker TikTok or a long-form upload, that is genuinely better than a flat block of text.

**Coverage.** Eleven platforms plus uploads against our one. If half your sources are YouTube or Instagram, a TikTok-only tool means running two workflows.

**Raw free capacity, in one burst.** 200 credits is roughly 400 minutes of transcription. If you have forty videos to get through this week, that beats two a day comfortably.

**Mind maps, summaries and chat.** We do none of this. A transcript plus a model you already pay for gets you there — see [how to summarise a TikTok video free](/summarize-tiktok-video-free) — but that is one more step, not zero.

## The thing you should check before signing up

WayinVideo's own pages disagree with each other about the free tier, and the gap is not small.

The **pricing page** says: `SIGNUP BENEFITS: 200 CREDITS (One-Time)`, with the comparison table listing "200 (one-time)" credits per year for the Free column and no recurring allowance.

The **TikTok transcript landing page** says: "200 Sign-Up Credits + 60 Daily Bonus", and separately, "Free to Start, No Login — no account, no credit card, no friction."

Those describe different products. A 60-credit daily bonus would be a recurring free tier roughly comparable to ours in shape; a one-time 200 is not. The export formats disagree too: the pricing table lists TXT and SRT, while the TikTok page promises TXT, DOC, PDF, SRT and VTT.

Marketing pages and billing pages drift apart on fast-moving products, ours included. But the pricing page is the one attached to the payment, so read that before planning a workflow around a daily allowance that may not exist. We made the same point about [Saveto AI's free but unpriced offer](/transcribetok-vs-saveto-ai): a generous claim with no matching commitment is a risk worth naming.

## The verdict

**Choose WayinVideo if** you are producing video. Clipping, reframing, burned-in animated subtitles, scheduling, multi-platform sources, speaker labels, an API — it does all of it and we do none of it. Accept the subscription, the monthly credit reset and the no-refund policy as the price of that.

**Choose TranscribeTok if** the thing you want is the words. Two a day forever, no account, no subscription, TXT, DOCX or SRT, done in about ten seconds. If you need more than two on a given day, $5 buys 150 transcripts that never expire.

**Choose neither if** you need volume through code. ## Frequently asked questions

**What is the main difference between TranscribeTok and WayinVideo?**
Scope. WayinVideo is a full AI video platform that clips, reframes, subtitles, summarises and publishes video from eleven source platforms including TikTok. TranscribeTok takes a single public TikTok link and returns the spoken text as TXT, DOCX or SRT. One is an editing suite, the other is a single-purpose utility.

**Is WayinVideo free?**
WayinVideo has a free plan, but the size of it depends on which page you read. Its pricing page states 200 credits granted once at signup, roughly 400 minutes of transcription, with no recurring free allowance. Its TikTok transcript landing page advertises 200 signup credits plus a 60-credit daily bonus and says no login is needed. Those two claims are not the same offer, so verify what you actually receive before planning around it.

**How much does WayinVideo cost?**
WayinVideo's list prices as of August 2026 are Starter $13.99 per month, Pro $26.99 per month and Scale $139.99 per month, with heavily discounted annual rates advertised at $4.99, $9.58 and $69.99 per month billed yearly. All plans are auto-renewing subscriptions, credits reset monthly without rolling over, and the FAQ states payments are nonrefundable.

**Does WayinVideo work on TikTok videos?**
Yes. WayinVideo accepts public TikTok links alongside YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch, Dailymotion, Rumble, Kick, Zoom and Google Drive, plus direct uploads. Like every tool in this category it cannot reach private, friends-only or deleted videos.

**Which tool should I use for TikTok subtitles?**
Either will give you a subtitle file, so pick on volume and terms. WayinVideo's pricing table lists TXT and SRT as its subtitle export formats on every tier, and its TikTok page additionally claims DOC, PDF and VTT. TranscribeTok exports TXT, DOCX and SRT, and does not export VTT, JSON or CSV.
