This page is for the second group. Choppity is a capable editor for cutting long videos into short, captioned clips. For pulling the text out of a public TikTok, a single-purpose transcript tool is simpler and cheaper, and that is what TranscribeTok is.

## What Choppity actually is

Choppity is an AI video editor built around clipping. You upload a long recording, such as a podcast or webinar, and its AI clipper picks highlights, cuts them into vertical clips, reframes them to 9:16 and adds animated captions. It also has a text-based editor, so you can cut video by editing the transcript.

Plans are sized in hours of AI video analysis: 1 hour on Free, 3 on Starter ($24 a month) and 5 on Creator ($32 a month) and Team ($48 a month), with roughly 30% off on yearly billing. Choppity's help centre says Free plan exports carry a Choppity watermark and that upgrading removes it.

**Where Choppity is clearly better:** turning one long video into many short clips; animated captions burned into your own footage; transcripts exported as SRT and VTT subtitle files as well as TXT; uploading your own video files, including ones not on TikTok. If you publish clips from podcasts or long videos every week, Choppity does a job TranscribeTok does not attempt. Our [TikTok subtitle generator guide](/tiktok-subtitle-generator) covers the captioning side in more detail.

## What happened when we tested the free TikTok transcript page

Choppity's "Free TikTok Video to Transcript Generator" page offers a box for a TikTok URL and an upload button, and its FAQ answers "Is the TikTok transcript generator free?" with "Completely free with no signup."

On 19 October 2026 we pasted a public TikTok link from TikTok's own account and clicked Generate Transcript. No transcript appeared on the page. The browser went to app.choppity.com/transcribe and then to Choppity's "Create account" page.

That does not make Choppity a bad product. Once you have an account, the free plan includes the transcription generator. But the page's "no signup" claim did not match what happened. We could not confirm whether transcribing a TikTok draws down the free hour of AI analysis, so we have not assumed either way.

## Cost if you need many transcripts

For transcripts alone, one-time credit packs cost far less per video than an editing subscription. For clipping and captioning, our credits buy nothing, so compare on the job you actually have.

| Need | Choppity | TranscribeTok |
|---|---|---|
| A few TikTok transcripts a week | Free plan, with an account | Free, 2 a day, no account to read |
| 100 TikTok transcripts | Starter, $24/month | $9 once |
| 500 TikTok transcripts | Starter or Creator, monthly | $16 once |
| 1,500 transcripts over a quarter | Three months of a paid plan | $34 once |
| Clips cut from a podcast | **Choppity** | Not possible |

A free tier that resets daily is the sharpest difference for light users. Choppity's free allowance is a fixed hour of analysis on an account; TranscribeTok's is two transcripts every day, indefinitely. [Free vs paid TikTok transcript tools](/free-vs-paid-tiktok-transcript-tools) compares how every free tier in this field is shaped.

Heavy users who need thousands of transcripts at once should not use either tool for that. TranscribeTok processes one link at a time and has no bulk mode. For a whole profile in one go, TokScript and GetTranscribe import in bulk, and [Apify's TikTok transcript extractor](/apify-alternative) handles lists of thousands.

## Verdict

**Choose Choppity** if you turn long videos into captioned short clips, or need SRT and VTT subtitle files from your own footage. It is the better product for that, and TranscribeTok does not compete.

**Choose TranscribeTok** if you want the spoken words from a public TikTok, yours or anyone else's, as text. It costs nothing for two a day with no account to read, and $9 for 100 after that, with no subscription.
