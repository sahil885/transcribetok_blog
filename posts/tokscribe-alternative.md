TokScribe gives away, for free, most of what this category charges for: bulk import of 50 videos, JSON export, HD video downloads and three AI tools. If you are comparing it against TranscribeTok on a feature checklist, TokScribe wins the checklist. This post explains what it costs you instead, and the two situations where TranscribeTok is still the right choice.

Everything below was verified directly on tokscribe.com on 7 September 2026.

## What TokScribe actually is

TokScribe is a free, Whisper-powered transcription tool for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Basic transcription runs without an account; signing in (also free) unlocks the AI tools, bulk import and HD downloads.

Its feature list is unusually long for a free product. Transcription in 50+ languages with word-level timestamps. Export as TXT, SRT, VTT or JSON, with bulk export as a ZIP. Bulk import of up to 50 URLs in one pass. HD video and cover-image downloads without watermarks. A Chrome extension that transcribes from the video page. And three AI tools: a Viral Hook Generator that returns 20+ hook variants, an AI Script Writer that rewrites a transcript into a script, and a Virality Analyzer.

The homepage frames all of this as "100% FREE — All Premium Features Included", against a stated value of $39.99/month, under a "LIMITED TIME OFFER" heading. There is no pricing page on the site.

## The thing the feature list does not tell you

**TokScribe publishes transcripts.** Its homepage carries a "Recent Transcriptions" feed showing the video title, the creator's handle, view and like counts, and the opening lines of the transcript itself. It offers full-text search across all transcripts, browse pages sorted by most viewed, most liked and longest, and a page per author.

That is a deliberate product decision, not a bug — the archive is what makes TokScribe's search feature work, and for competitor research it is genuinely useful. But it means the transcript you generate is not private by default. If you are transcribing a client's unreleased video, a private-adjacent account you have access to, or anything under an NDA, this is disqualifying on its own.

TranscribeTok does not publish transcripts. Paid accounts keep them in a personal library; free transcripts are simply returned to you.

## Where TokScribe is clearly better

**Bulk.** TokScribe processes 50 URLs in one batch and exports the lot as a ZIP. TranscribeTok processes one video at a time and has no batch mode, no multi-link paste and no plans for one. If you are building a dataset, TokScribe is the better tool and there is no way to argue otherwise.

**Export formats.** VTT and JSON are both on TokScribe's list and neither is on ours. JSON in particular matters if a script is going to consume the output — you get structure instead of having to parse a text file. Our [SRT to VTT conversion guide](/srt-to-vtt-converter) exists precisely because we do not export VTT.

**Multi-platform.** TokScribe covers Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts alongside TikTok. TranscribeTok is TikTok only.

**Video and cover downloads.** TokScribe returns the MP4 without a watermark and the cover image in original resolution. We return text.

**AI tools included.** Hook generation, script rewriting and virality analysis are built in. We do none of that; our position is that a transcript plus any chat model reproduces most of it, which we walk through in [using TikTok transcripts with ChatGPT](/tiktok-transcript-for-chatgpt).

## Where TranscribeTok is better

**Transcripts stay yours.** Covered above. This is the whole argument, and for a meaningful share of readers it decides the question by itself.

**The price is published and fixed.** TranscribeTok sells one-time credit packs: $5 for 150 transcripts, $12 for 500, $29 for 1,500, $59 for 4,000. Credits never expire, there is no subscription, there is a 30-day money-back guarantee while you have used fewer than 20 transcripts, and no credit is charged when a video turns out to have no spoken audio. TokScribe publishes no prices at all and describes its current terms as a limited-time offer — which is honest of them, but means you cannot plan around it.

**DOCX export.** TokScribe's four formats do not include Word. If the transcript's destination is a document rather than an editor or a script, DOCX saves a conversion step.

**Scale, for what it is worth.** TokScribe's homepage counters read 17K+ transcriptions and 621+ users. That is a young product. It is not a reason to avoid it, but it is a reason not to build a workflow that assumes it will still be free next year.

## The verdict

**Use TokScribe if** you are researching competitors at volume, you need JSON or VTT, you want the video file as well as the text, or you want AI hook and script tooling without paying for it. Nothing about transcribing public viral content requires privacy, and for that job TokScribe is the better free tool today.

**Use TranscribeTok if** the transcript must not appear on a public page, if you need DOCX, or if you are picking a tool you want to still cost the same in twelve months. Also use it if you simply want two transcripts today without creating an account — that path stays open indefinitely.

**Use both** if you are doing competitor research in bulk and client work privately. They are not really the same product.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is TokScribe free?**
Yes, TokScribe is free at the time of writing, including bulk import, HD downloads and its AI tools. Its own homepage labels this a limited-time offer against a stated $39.99/month value, and the site publishes no pricing page, so the free terms are not contractually fixed.

**Are TokScribe transcripts public?**
TokScribe operates a public archive. Its homepage lists recent transcriptions with the video title, author handle, view count and transcript text, and it offers full-text search across transcripts plus per-author pages. Treat anything you transcribe there as publishable.

**Does TokScribe do bulk TikTok transcription?**
Yes. Signed-in TokScribe users can paste up to 50 URLs at once on its bulk import page and export the results as a ZIP. TranscribeTok has no bulk mode and processes one video at a time.

**What is the best TokScribe alternative?**
For private, one-at-a-time transcripts with published pricing, TranscribeTok is the closest alternative: two free transcripts a day with no account, then one-time credit packs from $5 for 150 transcripts. For bulk and JSON output, TokScribe itself is currently the stronger free tool.

**Which is more accurate, TokScribe or TranscribeTok?**
Both transcribe the audio track rather than TikTok's caption data, and both land in the mid-to-high 90s on clear speech. Accuracy differences between speech-to-text tools are far smaller than the differences in what happens to your transcript afterwards.
