VexaScribe is a better transcription tool than TranscribeTok at almost everything, and it costs $2 a month to start. If you transcribe podcasts, interviews, meetings or uploaded files, stop reading and go use it.

This comparison exists for the narrower question: when you only need the spoken words out of public TikTok links, is a general transcription subscription the right tool? Below is what VexaScribe actually does, verified from its own site in September 2026, and the two places where a TikTok-only tool still wins.

## What VexaScribe is

VexaScribe is an AI transcription service built on hosted OpenAI Whisper Large-v3. It was previously called NovaScribe and rebranded under the same team, which is worth knowing if you find older reviews under the old name.

You give it audio or video three ways: upload a file, paste a link, or send a bot into a live call. Uploads cover MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC and OGG for audio and MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and WebM for video, with 20+ formats supported in total. Links can be YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or a direct media URL. The meeting bot joins Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams and transcribes the call as it happens.

What comes back is a transcript with automatic speaker detection — Speaker 1, Speaker 2 — and timestamps, editable in the browser, exportable to TXT, SRT, VTT, JSON and DOCX. On top of that sit AI summaries in six varieties (meeting, sales, lecture, podcast, interview, general) and translation of any transcript into 133 languages via Google Translate.

It supports 99 languages. VexaScribe's own 2026 testing puts accuracy at 91–95% depending on audio quality and speaker count, which they publish with a methodology page — an unusually honest disclosure in this category.

**And it does bulk.** Up to 50 files uploaded at once, processed in parallel rather than sequentially, downloaded as a single ZIP. TranscribeTok does not do this and has never claimed to.

Three details that matter more than the headline price:

**Minutes reset monthly.** VexaScribe's pricing page states plainly that minutes reset when your subscription renews. Unused minutes are gone. This is standard for the category and it is the opposite of how TranscribeTok's packs work.

**Minutes are rounded up.** A 47-minute podcast costs 47 minutes; a 20-second clip costs a full minute. For short-form video that rounding matters — a hundred 30-second TikToks cost 100 minutes, not 50.

**The meeting bot burns 3× minutes.** Because the bot holds an open connection for the whole call, a 30-minute meeting consumes 90 minutes of allowance. VexaScribe documents this clearly on the pricing page rather than burying it, which is to their credit.

Every plan includes the full feature set — speaker labels, 99 languages, exports, AI summaries, the meeting bot, encryption, cancel anytime. There is no feature gating between tiers, only volume.

## Where TranscribeTok is different

Two places, and only two. Everywhere else VexaScribe wins.

**The free tier has a different shape.** VexaScribe's free trial is 30 minutes, granted once. TranscribeTok's free tier is two transcripts every day, resetting daily, forever, with no account and no card. On a typical 30–90 second TikTok, two a day is roughly 730 transcripts a year against VexaScribe's 30 one-time minutes. If your actual need is "a couple of TikToks most days," the free tier covers it permanently and you never pay anyone.

This is the pattern across the whole category, and it is worth internalising before you compare any two tools on price: most free tiers are lifetime grants, a few reset. We laid the full set out in [free vs paid TikTok transcript tools](/free-vs-paid-tiktok-transcript-tools).

**Credits do not expire.** TranscribeTok's paid tiers are one-time credit packs, not a subscription — $5 for 150 transcripts, $12 for 500, $29 for 1,500, $59 for 4,000. Credits never expire, there is a 30-day money-back guarantee while you have used fewer than 20 transcripts, and no credit is charged when a video turns out to have no spoken audio. If your TikTok transcription is bursty — a research project, a content audit, then nothing for four months — a pack you bought once beats a subscription you forget to cancel.

## The verdict

**Use VexaScribe if** you transcribe anything other than TikTok — podcasts, interviews, lectures, uploaded video, meetings — or if you need speaker labels, JSON output, translation, summaries, or more than one file at a time. At $5 a month for 1,000 minutes it is priced below most of the category and the feature set is genuinely broad. It is the better product.

**Use TranscribeTok if** TikTok is the only source, you want the text without creating an account, and your volume is a handful of videos most days. That is the entire case for it, and it is a real case: the free tier costs nothing forever and there is no signup step between you and a transcript.

**Use both if** you post short-form and long-form. There is no rule against a $0 TikTok tool sitting alongside a $5 subscription.
