Most people find the TikTok they want on their phone. That's where they're watching. So it's a little absurd that most guides to getting a transcript assume you're sitting at a desktop.

You don't need to be. The whole thing works in a mobile browser, takes about fifteen seconds, and doesn't require installing anything.

## On iPhone

**Step 1: Tap the Share arrow.** It's on the right-hand side of the video, below the comment and heart icons.

**Step 2: Tap Copy link.** In the share sheet, it's usually in the top row of options. The TikTok URL is now on your clipboard.

**Step 3: Open Safari and go to [transcribetok.com](https://transcribetok.com).** Any browser works — Safari, Chrome, Firefox. Nothing to install.

**Step 4: Paste and tap Get Transcript.** Long-press the input box and tap **Paste**, then tap the button. The transcript appears in a few seconds.

**Step 5: Copy or download.** Tap Copy to put the whole text on your clipboard, or download it as TXT, DOCX or SRT. Downloads land in the **Files** app under Downloads.

## On Android

Identical, with slightly different wording:

**Step 1:** Tap the **Share** arrow on the right of the video.

**Step 2:** Tap **Copy link**.

**Step 3:** Open Chrome (or your browser of choice) and go to transcribetok.com.

**Step 4:** Long-press the input box, tap **Paste**, then tap **Get Transcript**.

**Step 5:** Copy the text or download it. Files go to your **Downloads** folder, reachable through the Files or My Files app.

## Making it faster

A few small things cut the number of taps:

**Use the share sheet to jump straight to your browser.** On both iOS and Android, the TikTok share sheet often lists your browser directly. Tapping it opens the link in the browser, and you can copy the URL from the address bar — skipping a step.

**Add transcribetok.com to your home screen.** In Safari, tap Share → *Add to Home Screen*. In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu → *Add to Home screen*. It then opens like an app, without being one.

**Keep a browser tab open.** If you're working through several videos in a sitting, leave the tab open and just paste each new link.

## Common mobile problems

**"Paste isn't appearing when I long-press."** Tap once in the input box first to focus it, then long-press. On iOS 17 and later you may also get a one-time "Allow Paste" permission prompt — tap Allow.

**"The link doesn't work."** Make sure you copied a video link and not a profile link. A video URL contains `/video/` followed by a long number. Short `vm.tiktok.com` share links work fine too.

**"Nothing downloads on iPhone."** Safari sometimes asks for permission the first time a site downloads a file. Check for a prompt at the top of the screen, and look in Files → Downloads rather than Photos.

**"The transcript is empty."** The video probably has little or no speech. Transcription works from the audio — if the message is entirely on-screen text or it's a dance clip, there's nothing to transcribe.
