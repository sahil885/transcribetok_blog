DownSub is one of the most-used subtitle downloaders on the web, and TikTok is on its list of supported sites. Paste a link, pick a format, download. For a lot of TikToks that is all you need.

The catch shows up on the videos that matter most often: the ones with no captions. We tested DownSub with three public TikToks to see exactly where it works, where it stops, and when an alternative is worth using.

## What DownSub actually is

**DownSub is a free online subtitle downloader for YouTube and more than 50 other video sites.** Its homepage lists TikTok alongside YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Bilibili, Viki, Instagram, X and others, and says it supports SRT, VTT and TXT with no extension or software. It also has a free Chrome extension and a "subtitle.to/" URL trick for YouTube links.

Its paid tier, DownSub Pro, adds features its plans page lists as: downloading multiple subtitles, auto-translating subtitles, playlist downloads, converting video or audio to subtitles, summaries, comment analysis and API access.

**Where DownSub is clearly better:**

- **Subtitle files.** It downloads SRT and VTT free. TranscribeTok makes no subtitle file at all.
- **Breadth.** One tool for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and dozens of streaming sites.
- **No account, no stated daily cap** on free caption downloads.
- **Translation and playlists** on Pro, for $5 a month.

## Our test: three public TikToks

**DownSub worked instantly on TikToks that had captions and returned nothing on one that didn't.**

1. **An @tiktok video (1:19).** DownSub showed the title, the duration and one track labelled "Eng-US", with SRT, VTT, TXT and RAW buttons. No sign-in.
2. **A second @tiktok video (1:24).** Same result: an "Eng-US" track, four formats.
3. **A 3:38 AI-music video from a smaller account.** DownSub returned "Sorry! Subtitles not found." and offered Pro instead: "This video has no subtitles - but DownSub Pro can create them from the audio with AI."

The third result is the important one. DownSub's free tool reads the caption track TikTok already stores. Not every TikTok has a caption track, and on those a free caption downloader comes back empty. The [TikTok captions download guide](/download-tiktok-captions) explains how TikTok's captions work and why the app can't export them.

We did not test DownSub Pro's audio transcription, and DownSub does not publish how many credits one TikTok uses.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does DownSub work with TikTok?**
Yes. DownSub lists TikTok among its supported sites, and in our test on 16 November 2026 it returned an English caption track for public TikToks with SRT, VTT, TXT and RAW download options and no account. For a TikTok with no caption track it showed "Sorry! Subtitles not found."

**Is DownSub free?**
DownSub's free plan downloads existing subtitles and transcripts with no account and no card. Its paid Pro plan costs $5 a month, with a 3-day free trial and 500 credits, and adds AI translation, playlist downloads, API access and creating subtitles from audio. A Lifetime plan costs $99 once with 50,000 credits.

**Why does DownSub say "Subtitles not found" for a TikTok?**
DownSub's free tool downloads caption tracks that already exist on a video. If the TikTok has no caption track, there is nothing to download, and DownSub offers its paid Pro plan to generate subtitles from the audio instead. A transcript tool that transcribes the audio, such as TranscribeTok, works on those videos for free.

**What is the best DownSub alternative for TikTok?**
For TikToks without captions, TranscribeTok is a free DownSub alternative: it transcribes the spoken audio of any public TikTok, two a day free with no account needed to read, and $9 for 100 after that. For TikToks that already have captions and when you need an SRT or VTT file, DownSub's free tool is the better choice.

**Can TranscribeTok download TikTok subtitles as SRT like DownSub?**
No. TranscribeTok downloads TikTok transcripts as TXT, DOC or PDF, with or without timestamps, and makes no SRT, VTT or other subtitle file. If you need a timed subtitle file and the TikTok already has captions, DownSub can download it as SRT or VTT.
