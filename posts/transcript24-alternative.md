Transcript24 offers more than TranscribeTok on paper: seven platforms, file uploads, an SRT-exporting extension and an API, with a core tool it describes as 100% free and no signup. If you transcribe anything beyond TikTok, it is the more capable tool.

This comparison is for the narrower question: if you only need the spoken words from TikTok links, which one should you use? Everything below was checked on transcript24.com on 9 October 2026, including its API documentation.

## What Transcript24 is

**Transcript24 is a free transcript generator built for content research.** Its TikTok page is framed around reverse-engineering viral videos: paste a top-performing TikTok, read its script, and study the hook and structure.

It takes three kinds of input:

- **A link** to a TikTok
- **An uploaded video file**
- **An uploaded audio file**

Beyond TikTok, it runs separate free transcript pages for **YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn and Pinterest**. It handles TikToks "typically up to 10 minutes", which covers almost everything on the platform.

There is also a **browser extension**: you open it on a video's page, the transcript appears in a sidebar, and you can copy it or **export it as an SRT subtitle file**. And there is a **transcript API** for developers.

TranscribeTok does none of the multi-platform, file-upload, extension or API work. It is a single-purpose TikTok tool and says so.

## How Transcript24 charges

**Transcript24 describes its core tool as completely free, with no signup.** Its FAQ says the TikTok generator "is completely free for everyday use" and that premium plans may come later only for "extremely high-volume needs". It states no daily cap.

Credits apply elsewhere, and the published rules are specific:

| What | Credit cost (Transcript24's own documentation) |
|---|---|
| API, existing platform captions | 1 credit per video |
| API, AI transcription | 1 credit per minute, rounded up |
| API, scene detection | 1 credit per minute, rounded up, even on caption results |
| Translation | 1 credit per 10 minutes of video, rounded up |
| Failed AI transcription | Not charged |

Two things are worth knowing before you build a workflow on it:

1. **No credit price is published.** There is no pricing page (`/pricing` returns a 404), so you cannot tell what a credit costs until you are inside the product.
2. **The documentation and the homepage pull in different directions.** The API page says "billing follows the same logic as the website", while the homepage says "100% Free". Both can be true if the free tier covers ordinary transcripts and credits cover translation and heavier work, but the site does not spell out where that line sits.

Neither point is a reason to avoid it. They are reasons to expect that "free" has a boundary you will discover in use rather than on a page.

## Where TranscribeTok is different

**TranscribeTok's pricing is fully published.** Two transcripts a day are free with no account. Beyond that, one-time credit packs cost $9 for 100, $16 for 500, $34 for 1,500 and $59 for 4,000, one credit per transcript whatever the length, and credits never expire. There is a 30-day money-back guarantee if you have used fewer than 20, and no credit is charged when a video has no spoken audio.

**Downloads are documents, not subtitles.** TranscribeTok lets you copy the transcript in one tap or download it as **TXT, DOC or PDF**, with or without timestamps. It makes no SRT or VTT file. If you need subtitles, Transcript24's extension is the better choice.

**It transcribes the audio track.** Like Transcript24's AI mode, TranscribeTok works on videos where the creator never enabled captions.

**Where we lose:** platforms, file uploads, SRT export, an API and, on Transcript24's own description, price. If your work touches YouTube, Reels or files on your computer, Transcript24 covers it and we do not.

## The verdict

**Choose Transcript24 if** you transcribe from more than one platform, have audio or video files to upload, need SRT subtitles, want an API, or simply want the most free use with no account. On its own description it does more than TranscribeTok for less.

**Choose TranscribeTok if** you only work with TikTok links and want every price, limit and refund term published before you start, or you want transcripts as DOC or PDF files to share. For a few TikToks a week, the free tier covers it.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Transcript24?**
Transcript24 is a web tool that turns TikTok videos into transcripts from a pasted link, an uploaded video file or an uploaded audio file. It also has free transcript pages for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Pinterest, a browser extension that can export SRT, and a credit-based transcript API.

**Is Transcript24 free?**
Transcript24 says its core TikTok transcript generator is completely free for everyday use with no signup, and handles videos up to about 10 minutes. It notes it may add premium plans for very high volume. Translations and its API use credits, and Transcript24 does not publish the price of a credit.

**Is Transcript24 better than TranscribeTok?**
For most uses, yes. Transcript24 covers seven platforms instead of one, accepts uploaded files, exports SRT through its extension, has an API and says its core tool is free with no daily cap stated. TranscribeTok is the better fit only if you want published, one-time pricing and a refund policy, or DOC and PDF downloads of TikTok transcripts.

**How does the Transcript24 API charge?**
Transcript24's API documentation charges 1 credit per video when it returns existing platform captions, and 1 credit per minute of video, rounded up, when it runs AI transcription or scene detection. Failed AI transcriptions are not charged. The documentation says website billing follows the same rules, but no credit price is published.

**Does Transcript24 export SRT subtitles?**
Yes, through its browser extension: Transcript24's setup page says you can copy the transcript or export it to an SRT file from the extension sidebar. TranscribeTok does not make SRT files; it gives you the text to copy or download as TXT, DOC or PDF.
