There are now dozens of TikTok transcript tools, and most comparison posts on the topic are thinly disguised ads for one of them. This one is written by the team behind TranscribeTok, so treat our own entry with the scepticism that deserves — including the part where we tell you to use something else.

**Every free tier, export format and price below was checked on the tools' own pages on 8 November 2026.** Free tiers in this category change often, so check the limit before you build a workflow on one.

## The three questions that decide it

Almost every tool does the same core job: paste a link, get text. The differences that sound impressive in marketing copy — "99% accurate", "2-second processing" — are mostly noise. Three questions actually separate them:

1. **Does it transcribe the audio, or only copy TikTok's captions?** This decides whether you get anything back from a video with no caption track.
2. **How many free transcripts, and do you need an account?** Free tiers run from one a day to "unlimited", and some only apply without an account.
3. **One video at a time, or batch?** Batch matters for research across dozens of videos and is overkill for the occasional clip.

If you need a subtitle file, add a fourth: **does it export SRT or VTT?** TranscribeTok does not.

## Caption readers vs audio transcribers

**The biggest practical difference between these tools is whether they transcribe the audio or only copy the captions TikTok already has.** A caption reader is fast, but when a video has no caption track it returns nothing. An audio transcriber runs speech recognition on the sound, so it works either way.

- **Caption readers.** TokConnect says plainly that its tool "does not listen to the audio". TokCaption returns a no-captions result when a video has none. Ziztu reads captions for free and charges credits to transcribe the sound. VexaScribe's free link tool also extracts captions; its AI transcription is for signed-in file uploads.
- **Audio transcribers.** TranscribeTok, Vook.ai, Kapwing, Choppity, Transcript.you and WayinVideo transcribe the audio track. Supadata falls back to AI transcription when a video has no captions.

If a caption reader comes back empty, the video has no caption track. Run it through an audio transcriber rather than retrying.

## Single-video tools

Good for: grabbing the script from a video you just watched.

**TranscribeTok** *(ours)* — Transcribes the audio, so it works on videos with or without captions. Two transcripts a day free, readable with no account. A free account unlocks one-tap copy and downloads as TXT, DOC or PDF, with or without timestamps — no SRT or VTT file. Paid credit packs are one-time (from $9 for 100 transcripts, credits never expire) and also save transcripts to a library. One video at a time.

**tokscript.org** — A separate site from TokScript at tokscript.com, despite the name. Free with no signup for videos up to five minutes, and exports SRT, VTT and TXT that keep the original timing. Longer videos, up to two hours, need a paid account; one-time packs start at $9 for 600 minutes. One task at a time.

**TokConnect** — Ten free searches with no signup, shared across all of TokConnect's tools rather than per day. Downloads TXT or SRT, with or without timestamps. It reads TikTok's captions only, so a video without them returns nothing. Paid plans start at $34.99 a month billed yearly.

**Ziztu** — Copying a video's existing captions is free and needs no account, and saves as TXT, SRT or VTT with timestamps you can switch off. Transcribing the audio of a video without captions uses Whisper, needs a signed-in account and costs credits. Plans start at $7 a month.

**Vook.ai** — One free transcription a day, and the first needs no account. Transcribes the audio track with speaker labels and timestamps, and exports PDF, DOCX, Markdown, SRT or HTML. Pro is $13 a month for 10 hours, or $3 an hour with no subscription.

**Kapwing** — Takes a TikTok link or an uploaded file and transcribes it with AI speech recognition. A free account gets 10 minutes of transcription a month; the page mentions TXT downloads and timestamps in its subtitles view. Worth it if you also want to edit the video.

**Choppity** — An AI clip editor whose free TikTok transcript page takes a link or an upload and transcribes the audio. The page says no signup; when we tested it, it asked us to sign up. See our [Choppity comparison](/choppity-alternative).

**Saveto AI** — No signup, very wide language coverage, an advertised unlimited free tier and no published pricing page.

**VexaScribe** — Quick turnaround with a good range of exports including SRT, VTT and JSON. Its free TikTok link tool copies TikTok's captions, one a day with no signup; AI transcription of the audio is for signed-in uploads.

**WayinVideo** — Exports to TXT, DOC, PDF, SRT and VTT, with quote extraction and in-transcript search on the page. Its pricing page and its TikTok page disagree on whether you need to log in.

**Transcript24** — Describes its core TikTok tool as completely free with no signup, handles videos up to about 10 minutes, accepts links or uploaded files, and covers six other platforms. No credit prices are published for its API or translations. See our [Transcript24 review](/transcript24-alternative).

**tiktoktranscript.io** — Minimal single-purpose converter, no frills.

For one video that has captions, most of these are close to interchangeable. Choose on whether it transcribes the audio, the export format you need, and whether you're willing to make an account.

## Batch tools

Good for: competitor research, content audits, transcribing a back catalogue.

**TokScript** (tokscript.com) — Free accounts get five transcripts a day, copied or exported as TXT, XML, JSON or CSV. Paid plans ($10 a month or $39 a year) remove the daily limit, take up to 50 links at once and import a creator's profile or a collection of up to 3,000 videos. Covers TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Full head-to-head: [TranscribeTok vs TokScript](/transcribetok-vs-tokscript).

**TokCaption** — Five transcripts a day on a free plan (no card), exported as TXT, SRT, VTT or CSV with timestamps. Batches of up to five links, and profile or collection imports of up to five videos, are free; paid plans ($9 a month or $69 a year) raise both to 50 and add JSON and PDF exports, and the annual plan includes an API. It reads captions only, so videos without them return nothing.

**TransClipper** — Bulk import of 50 videos on paid plans across TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus AI agents that break down hooks and virality, weekly creator tracking and an API. Its no-account free tier is 3 a day but only for videos up to 60 seconds. See our [TransClipper alternative comparison](/transclipper-alternative) for verified pricing.

**Transcript.you** — A YouTube-first transcription site whose TikTok tool transcribes the audio with Whisper. Paid plans ($9.99 a month, $79.99 a year or $29.99 a month) take up to 50 TikTok links per batch and export TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT and VTT; the free TikTok allowance is one video a day. See our [Transcript.you alternative comparison](/transcript-you-alternative).

**GetTranscribe** — Organises videos by competitor into folders and lets you query across a whole collection. Strong on the research-workflow side.

**Apify / Supadata** — API and scraping platforms rather than consumer tools. The right answer if you're a developer building transcription into a pipeline, and the wrong answer if you want a web page to paste a link into.

If you're transcribing fifty videos, use one of these. A single-video tool makes that workflow painful rather than slow, and no free tier will absorb it.

## Two different tools called TokScript

**tokscript.com and tokscript.org are separate sites with the same name.** tokscript.com is the batch tool above: a free account, five a day, TXT, XML, JSON and CSV exports, and subscriptions for volume. tokscript.org needs no account for videos up to five minutes, exports SRT and VTT, and sells one-time packs. If someone — or an AI assistant — recommends "TokScript", check which site the link goes to before you compare prices.

## What "free" usually means

Almost nothing here is unlimited and free. The common models are a daily cap (ours is two a day), a lifetime or monthly allowance (TokConnect's ten searches, Kapwing's ten minutes a month), a free tier that only covers captions (Ziztu), or a trial that converts. All of these are reasonable — transcription costs real compute — but "free TikTok transcript tool" should always be read as "free up to a limit."

The practical advice: work out your actual volume first. A couple of videos a week and any free tier works, so pick on convenience. Fifty videos a month and you're choosing a paid tool whether you like it or not, so pick on workflow instead.

## Accuracy: the honest version

Every tool in this category claims high-90s accuracy. Most are telling roughly the truth for the easy case and quietly ignoring the hard ones.

What actually determines accuracy on TikTok specifically:

1. **Music level.** A trending sound mixed loud over the voice is the single biggest factor, and no tool fully solves it.
2. **Speech speed.** TikTok delivery is fast and often sped up in editing.
3. **Slang, brands and names.** The most common errors across every engine.
4. **Code-switching.** Mid-sentence language switches are the hardest case, and common in Filipino, Arabic-English and Spanish-English content.

Caption readers inherit the accuracy of TikTok's own captions, which are auto-generated unless the creator edited them. Audio transcribers depend on their speech model. On clean audio the differences between mainstream tools are small; on a noisy clip they all struggle.

## How to choose

**One video, occasionally.** Any single-video tool. If it returns nothing, the video has no captions — use one that transcribes the audio.

**You need a subtitle file.** TranscribeTok does not export SRT or VTT. tokscript.org, TokCaption, Ziztu, VexaScribe, WayinVideo and Transcript.you export both; TokConnect and Vook.ai export SRT.

**A few videos a week, for repurposing.** A tool that saves your history, so you're not re-transcribing things you already did.

**Competitor or niche research at scale.** A batch tool: TokScript, TokCaption or GetTranscribe, or an API if you're technical. Don't try to do this one link at a time.

**Feeding an AI tool.** Anything with a clean TXT export. Avoid tools that only give you SRT — [timestamps waste context](/tiktok-transcript-for-chatgpt).

**Building software.** An API platform, not a consumer tool.
