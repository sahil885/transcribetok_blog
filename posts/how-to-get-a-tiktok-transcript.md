## How to get a TikTok transcript in four steps

**Paste the link into a transcript tool; it takes about ten seconds per video.** This is the method for almost everyone.

**Step 1: Copy the link.** In the TikTok app, tap the **Share** arrow on the right of the video, then **Copy link**. On desktop, copy the URL from the address bar. Both the long `tiktok.com/@user/video/...` form and the short `vm.tiktok.com/...` share link work.

**Step 2: Open [TranscribeTok](https://transcribetok.com).** It runs in the browser: no app, no extension, no install. Reading two transcripts a day needs no account.

**Step 3: Paste and click Get Transcript.** A typical short clip comes back in a few seconds. You only need the link; the video is never downloaded to your device.

**Step 4: Review, then copy or download.** Skim for names, brands and slang, then copy the text in one tap or download it as TXT, DOC or PDF, with or without timestamps. Copying and downloading need a free account.

It works on any public video, whether or not the creator ever turned captions on, because TranscribeTok transcribes the audio track rather than reading TikTok's caption data. ## Why TikTok's own captions don't solve it

**TikTok's auto-captions are a display feature, not a data feature.** They appear over the video while it plays, but there is no copy-all, no export and no way to select the text. Reading along is not the same as having the words.

| What you want to do | TikTok captions on screen | Extracted transcript |
|---|---|---|
| Read along while watching | Yes | Yes |
| Search for a phrase | No | Yes |
| Paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini | No | Yes |
| Save the script for later | No | Yes |
| Compare 20 videos side by side | No | Yes |
| Works when the creator never enabled captions | No | Yes |

The gap widens once you move past a single video. Watching twenty TikToks to find every hook a competitor uses takes an afternoon; reading twenty transcripts takes minutes, and you can search them.

## What a transcript captures, and what it misses

**A TikTok transcript captures what people say, and nothing else.** That is exactly right for talking-head content such as advice, tutorials, reviews and storytelling. It misses three things:

- **Text overlays.** Words the creator typed onto the video are rendered graphics, not text data.
- **Song lyrics.** Music under the voice is noise to a speech model and is not transcribed reliably.
- **Anything non-public.** Private accounts, friends-only posts and deleted videos are inaccessible to every tool.

If a transcript comes back mostly empty, the video almost certainly has little or no speech: a dance, a montage, or a clip whose message is all on-screen text. TranscribeTok charges no credit when a video has no spoken audio.

## Which download format to pick

**Choose TXT unless a person is going to edit or receive the file.** TranscribeTok downloads three formats, each with or without timestamps.

## How accurate is a TikTok transcript?

**Expect mid-to-high 90s accuracy for clear speech in a well-supported language.** That is good enough to search, summarize and quote after a quick read-through. Four things lower it, in rough order of impact:

1. **Loud background music.** A trending sound mixed over the voice costs accuracy no tool can recover.
2. **Speed.** TikTok delivery is fast and often sped up in editing.
3. **Overlapping speakers.** Duets and stitches are the usual culprits.
4. **Slang, brand names and code-switching.** Platform vocabulary and mid-sentence language switches are where errors cluster.

Read any line back against the video before you publish it as a quote. That applies to every automatic transcription tool.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I get the text from a TikTok video?**
Copy the video's link from TikTok's Share menu, paste it into a transcript tool such as TranscribeTok, and the spoken text appears in a few seconds. **Does TikTok have a built-in transcript feature?**
No. TikTok shows auto-captions on screen while a video plays, but there is no way to select, copy, export or download that text anywhere in the app or on the web. Getting the words as text requires a transcription tool that works from the video's audio.

**Do I need an account to get a TikTok transcript?**

**Can I get a transcript from a private TikTok?**
No. Only public videos can be transcribed. Private accounts, friends-only posts and deleted videos are not accessible to any transcript tool.

**Does a TikTok transcript include the text written on screen?**
No. A TikTok transcript captures the spoken audio only. Text overlays typed onto the video are rendered graphics, not text data, so a video whose message is entirely on-screen text returns an empty or near-empty transcript.
