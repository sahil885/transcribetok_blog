We have tried to write the comparison we would want to find, including the part most vendor comparisons leave out: on the single measure people care most about — how much you get for free — Saveto AI's offer is better than ours on paper.

## What each one actually is

**Saveto AI** (operated by SAVETO LTD) is an all-in-one transcription and summarisation platform. It takes links from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, files from Google Drive and Dropbox, and direct uploads of local video and audio. Around that sits a large second layer: summarizers for video, audio, images, plain text, PDF, Word and PowerPoint; study tools including a flashcard maker, quiz generator, homework helper, infographic generator, mind map generator, APA citation generator and study guide maker; an AI presentation builder; a podcast generator; a voice cleaner; and video and subtitle downloaders. Its TikTok page advertises 200+ languages, accuracy "up to 99.9%", no sign-up, and no usage limit. It has no public pricing page.

**TranscribeTok** takes one TikTok link and returns the spoken text. That is the whole product. No other platforms, no file uploads, no batch, no API, no browser extension. Two transcripts a day, free, with no account and no card. Exports are TXT, DOCX and SRT.

These are not competing versions of the same product. One is a suite; one is a utility. Most arguments about which is "better" are really arguments about how wide the job is.

## Where Saveto AI is clearly better

**Breadth of input.** If your material is not all TikToks, this is decisive. A single tool that handles a YouTube lecture, an Instagram Reel, a Zoom recording sitting in Drive and an MP3 from your phone is worth more than four tools that each handle one. TranscribeTok rejects everything that is not a public TikTok URL — no uploads, no audio files, no other platforms.

**The free allowance.** Saveto AI states its TikTok transcript generator is unlimited and requires no sign-up. We give you two a day. If you need ten transcripts this afternoon and do not want to pay anything, we are not the answer and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

**Everything after the transcript.** Summaries, key points, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps and slide decks are all built in. We have none of that. You can reproduce most of it by pasting the text into an AI tool yourself, which is what [using a TikTok transcript with ChatGPT](/tiktok-transcript-for-chatgpt) and [summarising a TikTok video free](/summarize-tiktok-video-free) both walk through, but that is a manual loop rather than a button.

**Document work.** PDF, Word and PowerPoint summarisation has nothing to do with TikTok and is genuinely useful if your week involves reading long documents. We do not compete here at all.

## What neither of them does

Three limits apply to both tools, and to every competitor in this category:

- **Public videos only.** Private accounts, friends-only posts and deleted videos are inaccessible to any transcription tool. There is no workaround.
- **On-screen text is not captured.** Text a creator types onto the video is a rendered graphic, not data. Transcription reads the audio track. If a video's substance lives in the text overlay rather than the voiceover, neither tool recovers it.
- **Neither replaces TikTok having an export.** TikTok generates automatic captions for playback but provides no copy, export or download option anywhere in the app or on the web, which is the entire reason this category exists. One point in our favour on the second bullet: we transcribe the audio track directly, so a transcript comes back whether or not the creator ever enabled TikTok's captions.
