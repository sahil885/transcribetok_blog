Transcript.you is a broader tool than TranscribeTok. It transcribes YouTube, TikTok and uploaded files, exports subtitle files, translates, and takes 50 links at a time. If you need any of that, it is the better choice and this page will say so.

This comparison is for the narrower question: if what you need is the spoken words from TikTok links, which one fits? Everything below was checked on transcript.you on 2 November 2026: its TikTok page, its bulk TikTok page and its pricing page.

## What Transcript.you is

**Transcript.you is a general transcription site with a TikTok tool attached.** Its homepage is a YouTube transcript generator, and its menus add audio and video file transcription, meeting and interview transcription, subtitle tools, downloaders and bulk pages for TikTok, Spotify, Vimeo and Twitch. It says it has more than 51,100 users.

Its TikTok tool works the way ours does in one important respect: it downloads the video's audio and transcribes it with OpenAI's Whisper, so it does not depend on the creator having switched captions on. Short `vm.tiktok.com` links and full video links both work.

What it does that TranscribeTok does not:

- **Subtitle files.** Exports SRT and VTT for loading into CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve.
- **Translation.** Paid plans translate transcripts into more than 130 languages.
- **Batches.** Bulk TikTok transcription takes up to 50 links in one go and returns files in TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT and VTT.
- **Other sources.** YouTube links, uploaded audio and video files, and links from several other platforms.

## How Transcript.you charges

**Transcript.you is a subscription with monthly credits; its free tier for TikTok is one video a day.** Its TikTok page sets out three levels:

| | Not signed in | Free account | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read and copy the transcript | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| TikToks per day | 1 | 1 | "Unlimited" |
| TXT, DOCX, PDF download | No | Yes | Yes |
| SRT and VTT | No | First one free | Yes |
| Translation | No | No | 130+ languages |
| Bulk, up to 50 links | No | Queue only | Yes |

The paid plans on its pricing page:

- **Creator:** $9.99 a month, 200 AI credits a month
- **Pro:** $79.99 a year (shown as $6.67 a month), 250 AI credits a month
- **Pro+:** $29.99 a month, 1,250 AI credits a month

Each plan offers a 7-day free trial that requires a card. The pricing page says one transcription costs 1 credit, summaries and other AI features cost 1 to 5, and credits refill every 30 days.

Two details on Transcript.you's own pages don't line up, and they are worth checking before you pay. The TikTok page calls paid use "unlimited" while the pricing page charges a credit per transcription. And the TikTok page says a free account downloads TXT, DOCX or PDF, while the pricing page's comparison table lists the free plan as TXT only, 5 credits a month and videos up to 5 minutes.

## Where TranscribeTok is different

**TranscribeTok does one thing: it turns a public TikTok link into text, and it charges once rather than monthly.**

- **Free tier:** two TikTok transcripts a day, resetting daily, readable with no account. Transcript.you's free TikTok allowance is one a day.
- **Account rule:** reading needs no account; copying or downloading as TXT, DOC or PDF needs a free account. A free account does not raise the two-a-day limit.
- **Paid:** one-time credit packs, not a subscription: $9 for 100 transcripts, $16 for 500, $34 for 1,500, $59 for 4,000. Credits never expire. Every pack saves transcripts to a library. There is a 30-day money-back guarantee if you have used fewer than 20 transcripts.
- **Exports:** TXT, DOC and PDF, with or without timestamps, plus one-tap copy. No SRT, no VTT, no translation.
- **Volume:** one link at a time. There is no bulk import.

TranscribeTok's $34 pack of 1,500 works out at about 2.3 cents and the $59 pack of 4,000 at about 1.5 cents, but you paste every link individually.

## The verdict

**Choose Transcript.you if you need subtitles, translation, YouTube or file transcription, or batches of 50.** It is a wider tool, and its bulk TikTok queue saves real time for agencies and researchers working through long lists.

**Choose TranscribeTok if you only transcribe TikToks and your volume comes in bursts.** You get two free a day instead of one, and when you pay, you pay once: no trial card, no renewal, no credits that reset at the end of the month.

If bulk is the deciding factor, also look at [TokScript](/transcribetok-vs-tokscript), which imports 50 links or a whole public collection, and [Apify](/apify-alternative) for lists of URLs at scale.

## Limits every tool shares

**No tool can transcribe what isn't spoken or isn't public.** Private, friends-only and deleted TikToks are out of reach for Transcript.you, TranscribeTok and everyone else. Text typed on screen over music is graphics, not audio, so a speech-to-text tool returns little or nothing for it. Accuracy drops for loud background music, sped-up voices and heavy slang on every Whisper-style system.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Transcript.you?**
Transcript.you is a web transcription tool that started with YouTube transcripts and now also transcribes TikTok links, uploaded audio and video files, and batches of up to 50 links. Its TikTok tool downloads each video's audio and transcribes it with OpenAI's Whisper, so it works on videos without captions, and it exports TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT and VTT.

**Is Transcript.you free for TikTok?**
Partly. Transcript.you's TikTok page says you can read and copy a transcript free without signing up, limited to one video a day, and that a free account adds downloads and one free SRT or VTT file. Its pricing page lists the free plan at 5 AI credits a month, videos up to 5 minutes and TXT-only exports, so check the current limits when you sign up.

**How much does Transcript.you cost?**
When checked on 2 November 2026, Transcript.you's Creator plan was $9.99 a month, Pro was $79.99 a year, and Pro+ was $29.99 a month, each with a 7-day free trial that requires a card. Plans include monthly AI credits (200, 250 and 1,250), and the pricing page says one transcription costs 1 credit.

**Is Transcript.you better than TranscribeTok?**
For many people, yes. Transcript.you covers YouTube, files and TikTok, exports SRT and VTT subtitles, translates into more than 130 languages and accepts 50 links at once. TranscribeTok is the better fit if you only transcribe TikToks, want two free transcripts a day instead of one, or prefer one-time credit packs that never expire over a subscription.

**Does TranscribeTok export SRT like Transcript.you?**
No. TranscribeTok gives you the transcript to copy or download as TXT, DOC or PDF, with or without timestamps, and makes no SRT or VTT subtitle files. If you need a subtitle file to load into an editor such as CapCut or Premiere, Transcript.you's paid plans export SRT and VTT.
