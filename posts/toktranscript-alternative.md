TokTranscript and TranscribeTok both do the same core job: paste a public TikTok link, get the spoken words back as text. The interesting differences are in the free tier, and specifically in two numbers and one word — 10 a month, 2 a day, and "public".

Everything below was verified directly on toktranscript.com on 7 September 2026.

## What TokTranscript is

TokTranscript is a TikTok transcript tool built around content research rather than plain extraction. The transcript is the entry point; the product is what it does with it afterwards.

The free plan gives 10 transcripts a month with word timestamps and TXT download, no login required to try, in 50+ languages, plus one Viral Breakdown and one Script Remix. Pro is $55 a year (about $4.60 a month, billed annually) for 50 transcripts a month, 50 Viral Breakdowns, 100 Script Remixes, SRT and DOCX export, and transcript history up to 30. Max is $168 a year (about $14 a month) for 250 transcripts, 250 breakdowns, 600 remixes and history up to 99. A one-time $0.99 Quick Pack buys 10 lifetime uses.

The AI features are the point of difference. **Viral Breakdown** dissects someone else's video — hook structure, emotional pacing, key phrases, the storytelling formula. **Hook Analyzer** scores your own hook 0–100, names the word that lost the viewer and offers three rewrites. **Script Remix** takes a viral video as a structural template and rewrites it around your topic. There is also bulk download of up to 20 videos, a Chrome extension, a thumbnail downloader, an AI summary and a mind map.

## The free-plan detail worth reading twice

**On TokTranscript's free plan, your content is public.** Its own pricing table has a "Content privacy" row: Free is "Public to Plaza", Pro and Max are "Private". Plaza is TokTranscript's browsable feed of community transcripts — 1,949 of them at the time of writing, each with the video, the handle, the full transcript and an AI summary.

That is disclosed clearly, which is more than some tools manage, and for public viral videos it costs you nothing. But it inverts the usual assumption: on TokTranscript, privacy is the upgrade. On TranscribeTok, free transcripts are simply returned to you and never published.

## Monthly caps versus daily resets

This is the single biggest practical difference between the two tools, and it is not close.

TokTranscript's free plan is **10 transcripts per month**. Spend them on the 3rd and you wait until the next month. TranscribeTok's free tier is **2 transcripts per day**, which resets every day — roughly 60 a month, or about 730 a year, and it never runs out in a way that blocks you tomorrow.

Neither shape is universally better. Ten in a burst beats two a day if you are doing one research session and never coming back. Two a day beats ten a month if transcribing a TikTok is a habit rather than a project. Most people who install a transcript tool are in the second group, which is why we keep making this argument in [free vs paid TikTok transcript tools](/free-vs-paid-tiktok-transcript-tools).

## Where TokTranscript is clearly better

**The AI research layer.** Viral Breakdown and Hook Analyzer have no TranscribeTok equivalent, and they are not trivial to reproduce by hand. You can approximate them with a transcript and a chat model — that is what [TikTok transcripts for ChatGPT](/tiktok-transcript-for-chatgpt) is about — but TokTranscript has done the prompt engineering and the scoring rubric for you.

**Bulk, up to a point.** 20 videos in one pass. TranscribeTok does one at a time, always.

**Mind maps and AI summaries.** Both are Pro features and both are things we do not offer. Our answer is the manual route in [summarise a TikTok video free](/summarize-tiktok-video-free).

**A Chrome extension.** TranscribeTok has none.

**Cheapest possible entry.** $0.99 for 10 uses is a lower first cheque than our $5.

## Where TranscribeTok is better

**Free allowance, by a factor of six.** 2/day against 10/month.

**Free transcripts are private.** No public feed, no community archive, no upgrade required to keep your own text to yourself.

**No subscription.** TokTranscript's Pro and Max are recurring annual plans. TranscribeTok sells one-time credit packs — $5 for 150, $12 for 500, $29 for 1,500, $59 for 4,000 — and credits never expire. There is a 30-day money-back guarantee while you have used fewer than 20 transcripts, and no credit is charged if a video turns out to have no spoken audio.

**Cost per transcript.** TokTranscript's Quick Pack works out at about 10c per video and its Pro plan at roughly 9c per transcript at the plan's cap. TranscribeTok's entry pack is about 3.3c and its largest pack about 1.5c. If all you want is text, we are between three and six times cheaper per unit.

**SRT and DOCX are not paywalled.** TokTranscript gates both behind Pro. If you need a subtitle file for CapCut or Premiere, that is a paid feature there and an included one here. The formats and their quirks are covered in [TikTok transcripts with timestamps](/tiktok-transcript-with-timestamps).

## The verdict

**Use TokTranscript if** you want the analysis and not just the words — hook scoring, viral breakdowns, script remixing — or if you need 20 videos in one batch. Its AI layer is the real product and it has no equivalent here.

**Use TranscribeTok if** you transcribe regularly rather than in bursts, if you want your transcripts to stay off a public feed without paying for the privilege, if you need SRT or DOCX without a subscription, or if you just want the lowest cost per transcript.

**A reasonable split:** free TranscribeTok for the everyday two, TokTranscript's $0.99 pack when you actually want a hook teardown. Neither requires you to commit to the other.
