Others typed "TikTok transcript", landed on Kapwing's transcript tool, and found a full video editor, a sign-in screen and a 10-minute monthly allowance standing between them and a block of text.

This comparison is for the second group. Kapwing is an excellent browser-based editor. If all you need is the words from a TikTok, it is more tool than the job requires.

## What Kapwing actually is

**Kapwing is a collaborative online video editor, and transcription is one feature inside it.** Its pricing page lists screen recording, templates, AI subtitles, dubbing, background removal, Smart Cut and brand kits, with team workspaces on paid plans.

Its TikTok transcript page says you can upload a file or paste a TikTok URL, handles videos from 10 seconds to two hours, claims up to 99% accuracy and removes filler words such as "um" automatically. Transcripts sync with the video, so deleting a sentence in the text deletes it from the clip. Subtitles can be translated, and the editor can dub into other languages.

**Where Kapwing is clearly better:** editing by transcript, burned-in subtitles, SRT and VTT export, translated subtitles, dubbing and team collaboration. If you are turning your own footage into finished social videos, Kapwing does a job TranscribeTok doesn't attempt. Our [TikTok subtitle generator guide](/tiktok-subtitle-generator) covers that kind of work.

## Kapwing's free plan, in detail

**Kapwing's Free plan gives 10 credits a month, usable for up to 10 minutes of auto-subtitling.** That allowance is shared with the rest of the editor's AI features, and it resets monthly, not daily.

The Free plan also exports with a Kapwing watermark, caps exports at 4 minutes and 720p, and keeps files for 3 days. The TikTok transcript page says a downloaded TXT transcript has no watermark. Kapwing's own pages are not fully consistent on that last point: the pricing table marks "Download video transcript" and "Download SRT/VTT files" as Pro features, while the TikTok transcript page says free accounts get TXT downloads. We have not tested which one applies, so treat free transcript downloads as possible rather than guaranteed.

For TikTok-length videos, 10 minutes goes further than it sounds: about 13 videos of 45 seconds. It still runs out early in the month for anyone researching more than a handful of creators.
