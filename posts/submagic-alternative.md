People search "Submagic alternative" for two different reasons. Some want a cheaper captioning editor. Others found Submagic's "free TikTok transcript generator" page, wanted the words from a TikTok, and hit a sign-up wall instead of a text box.

This page is for the second group. Submagic is a strong tool for captioning your own videos. It is not built for pulling the text out of someone else's TikTok, and that is the job TranscribeTok does.

## Submagic vs TranscribeTok at a glance

Submagic edits and captions videos you upload; TranscribeTok extracts the spoken text from a public TikTok link. They overlap only in the word "transcript".

## What Submagic actually is

Submagic is an AI video editor for short-form content. You upload a clip, it generates animated captions in a chosen style, and it adds B-roll, zooms and sound effects before you export a finished video. Its paid plans are sized in videos per month: 15 on Starter, 40 on Pro and 100 on Business, with maximum lengths of 2, 5 and 30 minutes.

It also produces subtitle files. Submagic's SRT generator page says you can "download clean, accurate SRTs", and says you can upload a video or paste a YouTube link. None of its pages we checked mention pasting a TikTok link.

**Where Submagic is clearly better:** styled, animated captions burned into your own videos; SRT export; B-roll and editing in one place; team seats; an API. If you publish short-form video every week and want it captioned, Submagic does a job TranscribeTok does not attempt. Our [TikTok subtitle generator guide](/tiktok-subtitle-generator) covers that side of the work in more detail.

## The "free TikTok transcript generator" page

Submagic ranks for TikTok transcript searches with a page titled "Free Online TikTok Transcript Generator". When we loaded it on 17 October 2026, the page had no input field: no box for a link and no upload button. Its two calls to action, "Try For Free" and "Get my FREE TRIAL", both pointed to app.submagic.co/signup.

The page's own FAQ says Submagic "is definitely free". The pricing page lists no free plan; it offers a trial with "No credit card required." A third-party pricing review (hooked.so, prices checked 25 September 2026) describes that trial as three short videos with watermarked exports. Submagic's own pricing page doesn't state the trial's size, so treat that figure as reported rather than confirmed.

None of this is unusual for a tool page that exists to capture search traffic. It does mean that if you arrived wanting the words from a TikTok, you are one sign-up and one upload away from a captioned video, which is not what you came for.

## The alternative: paste a TikTok link, read the text

TranscribeTok does one thing. You paste a public TikTok link, it transcribes the video's audio, and the full spoken text appears in a few seconds.

## Cost if you need many transcripts

The two tools price different jobs, so compare them on the job you actually have. If the job is reading what was said in other creators' videos, a credit pack is far cheaper per video than an editing subscription.

| Monthly need | Submagic | TranscribeTok |
|---|---|---|
| 10 TikTok transcripts | Starter, $19/month | Free over 5 days, or $9 once for 100 |
| 40 videos | Pro, $39/month | $9 once (100 transcripts) |

## Verdict

**Choose Submagic** if you make short-form videos and want them captioned, styled and exported, with SRT files and a team on one account. It is the better product for that, and TranscribeTok does not compete.

**Choose TranscribeTok** if you want the words from a public TikTok, yours or anyone else's, as plain text. It costs nothing for two a day, $9 for 100 after that, and needs no editor, upload or subscription.
