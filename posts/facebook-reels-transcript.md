Facebook will caption a reel for you. It will not hand you the words. There is no transcript panel, no copy button and no export option for viewers, which means every route to a Facebook Reels transcript runs through a third-party tool.

Stated plainly at the top: **TranscribeTok is a TikTok tool and does not transcribe Facebook videos.** The recommendations below are for other people's products, checked on their own pages in September 2026.

## Every Facebook video is a Reel now

A Facebook Reels transcript and a Facebook video transcript are now the same thing. In June 2025 Meta announced that all videos shared to Facebook would be published as Reels, removed the 90-second cap Reels previously had, and renamed the Video tab to the Reels tab, rolling the change out gradually to profiles and Pages.

That changes what you are transcribing. Reels used to be short by definition. Now a "reel" can be a 40-second clip or a 50-minute Page upload, and a tool priced per minute will charge very differently for the two. Check the length of what you are pasting before you pick a paid option.

## What Facebook gives you, and what it withholds

Facebook generates automatic captions using speech recognition, and viewers can make them show by default under Settings, then Media, then "Always show captions". Creators can switch auto-captions on when publishing.

None of that gives a viewer the text. Captions render as an overlay during playback: not selectable, not copyable, not downloadable. There is no Facebook equivalent of YouTube's transcript panel, which is the same position TikTok and Instagram take. We cover the TikTok side in [how to get a TikTok transcript](/how-to-get-a-tiktok-transcript) and the Instagram side in [our Instagram Reels transcript guide](/instagram-reels-transcript); the problem is identical on all three.

## The methods that work

### 1. A free web tool that supports Facebook

The fastest route for one-off use: paste the public link, get text back.

**Supadata's Facebook transcript page** is the lowest-friction option we found. One-off use is free with no login, heavy use is rate-limited, and it handles watch links, Page and profile videos, Reels and Live replays once the recording is published. It transcribes the audio rather than relying on Facebook's captions, and exports plain text or timestamped SRT. The same company sells the underlying API — see [our Supadata comparison](/supadata-alternative) for its credit costs.

**TokTranscript's Reels extractor** covers Facebook and Instagram reels, transcribes the audio with Whisper, and needs no login to try. It exports TXT and SRT. Its free tier is a monthly cap rather than a daily reset — details in [our TokTranscript review](/toktranscript-alternative).

### 2. An account-based transcript platform

Worth it if you transcribe Facebook video regularly and want a saved history or AI tools on top.

**WayinVideo** handles Reels, Stories, Live replays, Watch videos and standard posts in 100+ languages and exports TXT, SRT, VTT or DOC. A free account comes with 100 welcome credits. Our [WayinVideo comparison](/wayinvideo-alternative) covers how its credits work.

**GetTranscribe** handles Reels, public video posts, saved Live sessions and Watch videos, with plain text, timestamped text and SRT output. The free tier is 2 transcriptions in total, not per month, and requires an account. Pay-as-you-go is $0.06 per minute of audio, with subscriptions at $7.99 and $9.99 a month. More in [our GetTranscribe comparison](/transcribetok-vs-gettranscribe).

### 3. Download the file and transcribe it yourself

If the video is yours, or you have it as a file, skip the URL entirely. Use your original upload, or the open-source downloader yt-dlp, which supports Facebook links, then run the file through any transcription tool or a local Whisper model. This is the only method that works on a video you cannot reach by public link — and the only one where nothing leaves your machine.

For scripted volume, **Supadata's API** accepts Facebook URLs on the same endpoint as TikTok and Instagram, and **Apify** lists community actors for Facebook transcripts. Our [Apify review](/apify-alternative) explains how its per-minute billing works.

## How to pick

**Pick Supadata's free page** for an occasional reel with no account.

**Pick TokTranscript** if you work across Facebook and Instagram reels and want one tool for both.

**Pick WayinVideo or GetTranscribe** if Facebook video is a regular part of your work and you want saved transcripts and AI summaries. Check whether you are paying per minute: since Reels lost their length cap, one long Page video can use more allowance than twenty short clips.

**Pick download-and-transcribe** for long videos, sensitive material or your own uploads.

## What none of these can do

**Private and friends-only video.** Every URL-based tool fetches publicly. Friends-only posts, private groups and login-gated video are inaccessible everywhere. GetTranscribe says so explicitly; the others behave the same way.

**On-screen text.** Burned-in captions, stickers and text overlays are pixels, not data. No transcript tool reads them, on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok.

**Perfect accuracy.** Clear speech transcribes well. Music beds, crosstalk, fast delivery and slang all degrade it, and reels are often music-heavy. Read proper nouns before quoting.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you get a transcript of a Facebook Reel?**
Not from Facebook itself. Facebook can generate captions for reels and display them during playback, but it gives viewers no transcript panel and no way to copy, download or export the caption text. Getting a Facebook Reels transcript as text requires a third-party tool that works from the reel's public URL.

**Are all Facebook videos Reels now?**
Yes. In June 2025 Meta announced that all videos shared to Facebook would be published as Reels, removed the previous 90-second limit on Reels, and renamed the Video tab to the Reels tab, rolling the change out gradually to profiles and Pages. A Facebook Reels transcript tool is therefore effectively a Facebook video transcript tool.

**What is the best free Facebook Reels transcript tool?**
For occasional use, Supadata's free Facebook transcript page is the lowest-friction option: one-off use needs no login, it transcribes the audio rather than relying on Facebook's captions, and it exports TXT or SRT. TokTranscript's Reels extractor also needs no login to try. GetTranscribe's free tier is 2 transcriptions in total and requires an account.

**Does TranscribeTok work with Facebook Reels?**
No. TranscribeTok transcribes public TikTok videos only, one at a time, and will not accept a facebook.com URL. For Facebook Reels, use a multi-platform tool such as Supadata, TokTranscript, WayinVideo or GetTranscribe instead.

**Can you transcribe a private or friends-only Facebook video?**
No. Facebook transcript tools fetch the video from its public URL, so friends-only posts, private groups and anything that requires a login cannot be reached. GetTranscribe states this explicitly. If the video is your own, transcribe your original video file directly instead.
